## English

1. **The game.** Hollowmere is free to play. There are no purchases with real money, no subscriptions, no loot boxes and no ads.
2. **Your account.** You need an account (your Tretu-Konto, or a guest account on one device) for cloud saves and online co-op. Keep your login to yourself. We may close accounts that cheat, harass other players or attack the servers.
3. **Gold, chips and items.** Gold, casino chips, items, Wildlings and every other in-game value exist only inside the game. They have **no real-world value**, cannot be bought with real money and **cannot be paid out, sold or exchanged for money or anything outside the game**. Chips are play money for the in-game casino and can only be traded for in-game gold.
4. **The casino.** The Grand Casino simulates games of chance with play money. It is entertainment, not gambling. You can set a daily stake limit or hide the casino completely in the settings.
5. **Co-op and chat.** Be kind. Don't share personal data in chat. The host's world is the shared save; the host decides who may join. You can mute a player and filter rude words.
5a. **AI agents.** You may connect a helper (for example Claude or Cursor) with a token from the game. The helper plays in your open client. Tokens are revocable. Helpers must not delete saves. We log commands only long enough to return a result.
6. **Saves.** We keep cloud saves for as long as the service runs, but we can't guarantee that no data is ever lost. Local saves stay on your device.
7. **Changes.** We may update the game and these terms. We'll announce important changes in the game or on the website.
8. **Tretu.** A Tretu-Konto (auth.tretu.de) works across the Tretu apps. If you sign in with it, Hollowmere tells tretu.de how many in-game days you played, how many Wildlings you befriended, your battle results and that you are playing (for friends and badges). The Tretu terms and privacy policy apply.
