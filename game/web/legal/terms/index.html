The game

Hollowmere is free to play. There are no purchases in the game. Offline play on your own device does not need an account. Signing in is for cloud saves and playing together.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens through your account. A guest account stays on the device that created it until you add an email to it. Google, Apple and Discord each open their own account.

Saves

The game writes a save on your device. While you are signed in it also uploads that save, unless you turn cloud backup off. Cloud saves are there so you can continue on another device. We run the server with care, and we do not promise that a save can never be lost. Deleting a farm in the game deletes the local copy and the cloud copy for that slot.

Das Spiel

Hollowmere ist kostenlos. Im Spiel gibt es keine Käufe. Offline auf dem eigenen Gerät brauchst du kein Konto. Die Anmeldung ist für Cloud-Speicherstände und gemeinsames Spiel.
