# German glossary

UI labels, menus, tips and hints use the terms German players know from other games.
Dialogue may stay natural (a villager can hand you a "Karte" or talk about a Warden's "Team").
`tests/unit/test_i18n_glossary.gd` fails on the old dictionary-style translations.
